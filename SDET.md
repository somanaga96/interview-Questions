### 1.**Smoke Testing**
Purpose: To check whether the major functionalities of the application are working properly after a new build is deployed.
### 2. **Sanity Testing**
Purpose: To validate that the **new changes or bug fixes** are working as expected and haven't broken related functionality.
### 3. **Regression Testing**
Purpose: To ensure that recent code changes (new features, bug fixes, updates) haven’t negatively impacted existing functionality.

**Real-World Scenario**
Suppose you're testing an e-commerce application:

**Smoke Testing:** Ensure that the app launches, users can log in, view products, and place orders.
**Sanity Testing:** After fixing a bug in the checkout process, verify that the issue is resolved and payment functionality still works.
**Regression Testing:** After adding a new discount feature, verify that all existing functionality (cart, checkout, payment, etc.) works correctly.
